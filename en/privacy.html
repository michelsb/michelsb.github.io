Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how data may be handled when you use Michel Sales Bonfim's personal website, available at michelsb.github.io.

Data collected by this website

This is an informational static website. It does not provide forms, user accounts, comments, or subscriptions, and it does not intentionally use its own audience analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. The site does not set its own cookies for these purposes.

Third-party services

The website uses resources hosted by third parties. The profile photo is loaded from GitHub, and typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When you visit pages that load these resources, your browser may send technical data to those providers, such as your IP address, browser information, and requested page. The providers' privacy policies govern their handling of this data:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy

Links to UFC, Lattes, ORCID, and GitHub repositories lead to external websites. When you visit them, their privacy policies and practices apply.

Hosting

This website is published using GitHub Pages. The hosting infrastructure may process technical access data to operate, secure, and maintain the service, in accordance with GitHub's policies. This website does not have access to internal hosting logs.

Lattes CV

The CV provided on this website is a static PDF file. When you open or download it, your browser requests the file from the website's host, which may process technical access data as described above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the content of this website, please use the contact information available on the UFC institutional profile.
