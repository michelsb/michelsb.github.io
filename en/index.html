01 — About

Technology connecting
ideas and people.

I am a Computer Science professor at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Quixadá Campus. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), as well as a bachelor's and a master's degree in Computer Science from UFC.

As part of my academic training, I also completed a visiting research stay at the University of Ottawa in Canada. My work focuses on computer networks and distributed systems, connecting academic research with real-world digital infrastructure challenges.
